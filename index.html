Cube (مکعب روبیک)

The Rubik's Cube is a 3D combination puzzle invented in 1974 by Hungarian sculptor and professor Ernő Rubik. Originally called the Magic Cube, the puzzle was licensed by Rubik to be sold by Pentangle Puzzles in the UK in 1978, and then by Ideal Toy Corp in 1980. The cube was released internationally in 1980 and became one of the most recognised icons in popular culture. It won the 1980 German Game of the Year special award for Best Puzzle. As of January 2024, around 500 million cubes had been sold worldwide, making it the world's best-selling puzzle game and bestselling toy.
